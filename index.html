Miniproject: Sensory dissonance across timbres

Marius Qvarnstrom

I will implement Sethares' sensory dissonance model and test it against pleasantness ratings from a small listening test. The intervals from unison to octave are played with a harmonic and an inharmonic (bell-like) timbre. I expect the model to fit the ratings better for the harmonic timbre, because familiarity with Western intervals also affects what people like.
